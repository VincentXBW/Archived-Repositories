Job Tutor is a dedicated, not-for-profit organization committed to empowering individuals by breaking down the often intimidating barriers of the modern job market. With a strong core focus on employment education, the organization provides job seekers with the practical tools, personalized pathways, and step-by-step career roadmaps needed to confidently navigate their professional journeys. By focusing on fundamental skills and structured guidance, the organization ensures that everyone—regardless of their starting point—has access to the foundational knowledge required to understand the value of employment and achieve financial independence.
